    Import from a spreadsheet Add or update items from a CSV or Excel file. Rows match your items by code, or by name when there's no code, and empty cells change nothing. Columns: Code, Item, Category, Supplier, Unit, On hand, Low stock level, Cost, Cost currency, Price (or Price GBP / USD / EUR / ZAR) and Notes. You see every change before it's saved.

    Your WooCommerce shop Brings in your products' names, codes, categories, prices, stock levels and pictures. Invaro only reads from your shop, and the key stays on this computer.

    Needs a read-only REST API key In WordPress: WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API → Add key. Choose Read permissions, then copy the consumer key and secret here.

    Stock on invoices Saving an invoice takes its stock items off what's on hand. Editing it changes only the difference, and removing it from History puts them back. Quotes take nothing until they become an invoice.

    Ask Invaro with Gemini Optional. Ask Invaro answers what it can on this computer. With your own Gemini key, anything else goes to Google's Gemini with a summary of your clients, invoices, stock and figures, so it can answer many more questions. The key stays on this computer and isn't synced or shared with your team.

    Get a free key from Google AI Studio and paste it here. On Google's free tier, Google may use what's sent (including client names and amounts) to improve its products, and people may read it. A key from a Google project with billing turned on keeps it private.

    Updates Invaro checks GitHub () every 30 minutes and shows a bar at the top when an update is ready. Each download is checked against GitHub's checksum before it's installed.
