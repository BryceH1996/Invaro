Simple invoices for the clients you bill every month

Invoicer is a small, free app for Windows and Mac. Pick a client, fill in your days or hours, and save a clean PDF invoice. Invoicer remembers the rest.

Download for Windows or Mac How to install

What it does

Saved clients

Each with its own currency, hourly and daily rates, and payment terms.

Priced for you

Days, hours, days off or fixed amounts, worked out from the client's rates, with VAT if you charge it.

Six designs

Pick a template and colour, and add your logo, VAT number and EFT banking details.

Reminders

Your computer reminds you when it's time to invoice each client, and when an invoice is overdue.

Quotes and reports

Send quotes that become invoices, and see what you've invoiced, been paid and owe in VAT.

Your data, your choice

Everything is kept on your computer. Sign in with Google to back it up and use it on your other computers.

Is it safe?

Every release lists a SHA-256 checksum and a VirusTotal scan for each download, and all of the source code is public on GitHub. Invoicer has no ads, tracking or analytics. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it stores and where.
