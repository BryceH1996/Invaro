Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Invoicer is a desktop app for making invoices. This page explains what information it handles, where that information is kept, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your computer unless you choose to sign in, and if you do, it's kept in your own private account and used only to sync it between your computers.

If you don't sign in

Everything you enter (your details, your clients, your invoices, your logos and banking details) is saved as files in the Invoicer folder in your Documents, or a folder you choose. Nothing is sent to us. Invoicer has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Invoicer does connect to the internet in two ways, neither of which sends your data:

  • Updates: it asks GitHub whether a newer version of Invoicer is available.
  • Google Drive backups (optional): if you have Google Drive for desktop installed, Invoicer copies your saved PDFs into your own Google Drive folder, and Google Drive uploads them as usual. Invoicer itself never signs in to Google Drive.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets you back up your data and use it on more than one computer.

WhatWhy
Your name, email address and Google account ID, from Google when you sign inTo create your account and show who you're signed in as. Invoicer doesn't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
Your clients, your details and settings (including your VAT, tax and banking details), your invoice history, and your logosTo keep a copy in your account and sync it to your other computers.

Your PDF invoice files are not uploaded; they stay on your computer.

Your account data is stored with Supabase, which provides Invoicer's database and sign-in. It's sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each signed-in person read and change their own data. We don't sell, share or analyse your data, and we don't use it for anything other than syncing it back to you.

Your clients' information

The client details you enter (names, contact people, addresses, phone and VAT numbers) are information about other people and businesses. You decide what to enter; Invoicer only uses it to make your invoices and, if you sign in, to sync it between your computers.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • On your computer: your files are yours. Delete the Invoicer folder in your Documents to remove them.
  • In your account: in Invoicer, go to My details → Account & sync → Delete my cloud data. This removes your synced data from our database straight away (and your account too, where available) and signs you out. Your files on your computer are kept.
  • Signing out stops syncing but leaves your account data in place until you delete it.

We keep your account data only while your account exists.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, or the GDPR in the UK and EU), you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how it's used. You can do most of this yourself in the app; for anything else, contact us using the link below.

Children

Invoicer is meant for people running a business and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date, and significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Contact

Invoicer is made by BryceH1996 on GitHub. To ask about your data, contact the developer through that GitHub profile.
