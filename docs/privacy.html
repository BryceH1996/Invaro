Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (Ask Invaro with Gemini)

Invaro is a desktop app for making invoices. This page explains what information it handles, where that information is kept, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your computer unless you choose to sign in, and if you do, it's kept in your own private account and used only to sync it between your computers.

If you don't sign in

Everything you enter (your details, your clients, your invoices, your logos and banking details) is saved as files in the Invaro folder in your Documents, or a folder you choose. Nothing is sent to us. Invaro has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Invaro connects to the internet in these ways, none of which sends your data to us:

  • Updates: it asks GitHub whether a newer version of Invaro is available.
  • Exchange rates (optional): with rand totals turned on in Reports, it reads the public exchange-rate pages of Standard Bank, Absa and Nedbank for the days your invoices in other currencies were paid. Only a date is sent, never your invoices or details.
  • Importing from another app: spreadsheets and old PDF invoices you import are read on your computer. Nothing is sent to QuickBooks, Xero or anyone else.
  • Ask Invaro: the assistant is built into the app and works it out on your computer. Your questions and your data are never sent anywhere, unless you turn on Gemini (below).
  • Ask Invaro with Gemini (optional, off unless you add a key): if you add your own Gemini API key in My details, questions the assistant can't answer itself are sent to Google's Gemini service, with a summary of the business data needed to answer them: your clients' names and terms, your invoices (numbers, dates, clients, amounts and paid status), your stock and your totals. Team members who can't see money figures send no amounts. It goes straight from your computer to Google under your own key, never through us. On Google's free tier, Google may use what's sent to improve its products, and people may review it; with a key from a Google project with billing turned on, it doesn't. See Google's Gemini API terms. The key stays on your computer, encrypted, and isn't synced or shared with a team. Turn it off in My details at any time.
  • Your WooCommerce shop (optional): if you connect your shop on the Stock tab, Invaro reads your products from your own website, over HTTPS, with a read-only key you create. The key stays on your computer, encrypted, and is never sent anywhere else or shared with a team. Invaro never changes anything on your shop. Product pictures are downloaded from the addresses your shop gives, without the key.
  • Backups (optional): if you have Google Drive for desktop or OneDrive installed, Invaro copies your saved PDFs into your own Google Drive or OneDrive folder (or any other folder you choose), and that app uploads them as usual.
  • Connect Google Drive (optional): see below.

If you connect Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional, and separate from signing in. It lets Invaro back up your invoices to Google Drive on a computer without Google Drive for desktop.

  • What Invaro asks for: access to your Google Drive. Google requires full Drive access for an app to save into a shared drive, so that's what the consent screen shows.
  • What Invaro does with it: it lists your shared drives and folder names so you can choose where backups go, creates that folder ("Invaro Backups" unless you pick another) with a folder per client, and uploads or replaces the invoice PDFs you save. If you use Share your invoices, it shows who that folder is shared with and adds or removes the people you choose; Google then emails them a link. It doesn't read, change or delete any other files.
  • Where it goes: straight from your computer to your Google Drive. The PDFs and the access token are never sent to us or anyone else. The token is stored on your computer, encrypted by your operating system.
  • Stopping: Disconnect Google Drive in Invaro's settings deletes the token and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove Invaro at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in your Drive.

Invaro's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets you back up your data and use it on more than one computer. Accounts are invite-only for now: if you ask for access, your email address is added to a list of approved addresses, which only the developer can see.

WhatWhy
Your name, email address and Google account ID, from Google when you sign inTo create your account and show who you're signed in as. Invaro doesn't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
Your clients, your details and settings (including your VAT, tax and banking details), your invoice history, and your logosTo keep a copy in your account and sync it to your other computers.

Your PDF invoice files are not uploaded; they stay on your computer.

Your account data is stored with Supabase, which provides Invaro's database and sign-in. It's sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each signed-in person read and change their own data. We don't sell, share or analyse your data, and we don't use it for anything other than syncing it back to you.

If you work in a team

Teams are optional. If you create or join a team, your business's clients, invoice history and business details (including your logos and banking details) are kept in the team's shared space with Supabase instead of only your own account.

  • Who can see it: it depends on the role an admin gives each person. Admins see and change everything. An accountant sees all of the team's data but only marks invoices paid. A team member sees the clients and business details, but only the invoices and quotes they made themselves. Nobody outside the team can see any of it.
  • Who's in it: everyone in the team can see each other's email addresses and roles. When an admin invites someone, their email address and the role they'll get are stored until they join or the invitation is cancelled.
  • What stays on your computer: your PDF files, your backup settings, the invoice you're in the middle of typing, and any client or invoice you mark Personal. For a personal invoice, only its number is stored in the team (so nobody else uses the same number), never its details.
  • Leaving: leaving the team, or being removed, stops syncing; the copy already on your computer stays there. An admin can delete the team, which removes the shared data from the cloud for everyone.

Your clients' information

The client details you enter (names, contact people, addresses, phone and VAT numbers) are information about other people and businesses. You decide what to enter; Invaro only uses it to make your invoices and, if you sign in, to sync it between your computers.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • On your computer: your files are yours. Delete the Invaro folder in your Documents to remove them.
  • In your account: in Invaro, go to My details → Account & sync → Delete my cloud data. This removes your synced data from our database straight away (and your account too, where available) and signs you out. Your files on your computer are kept.
  • Signing out stops syncing but leaves your account data in place until you delete it.

We keep your account data only while your account exists.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, or the GDPR in the UK and EU), you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how it's used. You can do most of this yourself in the app; for anything else, contact us using the link below.

Children

Invaro is meant for people running a business and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date, and significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Contact
